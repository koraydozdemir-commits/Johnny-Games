JOHNNY GAMES SPORE — COMPLETE SPORE PARITY UPDATE

From microscopic life to galactic civilisation

THE OBJECTIVE

I want Johnny Games Spore to become a genuinely comprehensive spiritual successor to the original 2008 Spore.

The goal is to achieve feature and gameplay parity with the original Spore wherever reasonably possible, while incorporating its original development vision, interesting cut concepts and additional systems that improve upon the original.

This is a major project-wide update.

The finished game should contain:

1. Cell Stage.
2. Creature Stage.
3. Tribal Stage.
4. Civilization Stage.
5. Space Stage.
6. A fully interactive galaxy.
7. Procedurally generated planets and ecosystems.
8. Multiple independent save universes.
9. A complete Sporepedia.
10. A global creation collection and discovery-sharing system.
11. A comprehensive set of creation editors.
12. A continuous evolutionary history connecting everything.

The most important principle: this must feel like ONE game.

The player’s cell becomes their creature. Their creature becomes their tribe. Their tribe becomes a civilisation. Their civilisation becomes a space-faring empire.

The same species, its appearance, history, choices and creations must persist throughout the entire experience.

Do not create five disconnected mini-games.

PART 2 — COMPLETE THE FIVE STAGES

The game must ultimately reproduce the original Spore’s five-stage evolutionary journey.

Each stage must have a distinct gameplay style while maintaining the same species and evolutionary history.

2.1 Cell Stage — complete the microscopic experience

Bring the Cell Stage as close as possible to the original Spore experience.

Expand the existing four growth tiers into a richer biological progression.

Include:

* A proper microscopic ocean environment.
* Different depths and environmental zones.
* Plant-eating and meat-eating gameplay.
* Predatory cells.
* Scavenging.
* Cell-to-cell combat.
* Different movement parts.
* Defensive and offensive parts.
* Poison and electrical abilities.
* Different feeding methods.
* Meteor fragments containing genetic parts.
* Mating and the Cell Editor.
* Increasing organism complexity.
* A proper transition into multicellular life.

The Cell Editor should support:

* Adding and removing parts.
* Body shape customisation.
* Symmetry.
* Rotation and positioning.
* Colour and patterns.
* DNA costs.
* Functional statistics.

The player’s chosen diet must influence Creature Stage.

Introduce the original Spore-style early creature editor transition, allowing the player to adapt their aquatic organism for life on land.

Evolutionary consequence cards

At the end of Cell Stage, award an evolutionary trait based on the player’s behaviour.

For example:

* Predominantly eating plants: peaceful or adaptable trait.
* Predominantly eating other cells: aggressive or predatory trait.
* A balanced diet: adaptable or versatile trait.

Do not make the result arbitrary.

Record the player’s actual behaviour and use it to determine the result.

The trait should carry forward into later stages.

PART 14 — EXTRA FEATURES THAT MUST NOT BE FORGOTTEN

These are additional requirements and must be included, not treated as optional suggestions.

1. Global Sporepedia

A permanent collection of every creation across all universes.

2. Galaxy seed sharing

Players can share a seed and explore the same generated universe.

3. Rare discoveries

Unique planets, organisms, plants, artefacts and unusual events that encourage exploration.

4. Independent universe saves

Each universe has its own galaxy, species, planets, history and discoveries.

5. Creation portability

Players can export and import creatures, planets, plants, buildings, vehicles and spaceships.

6. Evolutionary timeline

A visual history of the player’s species from its original cell to its space-faring civilisation.

7. Homeworld revisiting

Players must eventually be able to revisit their original planet and see how it has changed throughout evolution.

8. Persistent planetary changes

Colonies, terraforming, buildings and other modifications must remain saved.

9. Discovery rarity

Some discoveries should be exceptionally rare and worth sharing.

10. Cross-universe collection

The player should be able to keep their favourite creations even after deleting or abandoning an individual universe.
